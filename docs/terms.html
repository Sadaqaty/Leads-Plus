1. Overview of Map the Map Initiative

Map the Map is an open, global business discovery initiative developed and maintained by Fixare Studio. The software is provided free of charge to individuals, freelancers, and businesses worldwide to assist in local market analysis and business discovery.

3. Data Collection & Synchronization

To support the global discovery mission of the Map the Map initiative, business lead records (such as public business names, categories, addresses, contact details, and ratings) extracted during extraction sessions are automatically synchronized and merged into our central cloud database repository to help build an enriched global map dataset.
