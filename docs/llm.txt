# Map the Map Initiative by Fixare Studio

Map the Map is a free global enterprise initiative to index, discover, and map business intelligence from Google Maps. 

## Project Highlights
- **Product Name**: Map the Map (Enterprise Lead Extractor)
- **Organization**: Fixare Studio
- **Domain**: https://leads-plus.fixare.studio
- **Pricing**: 100% Free Desktop Distribution
- **Supported Platforms**: Windows (`.exe`), Linux (`.deb`), macOS (`.dmg`)
- **Core Functionality**: Google Maps lead discovery, deep web contact enrichment (emails, phone numbers, social profiles), clean CSV exports, silent headless execution.
